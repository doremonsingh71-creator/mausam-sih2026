What does Doppler Radar do in Mausam? (SIH Guide)

1. Reflectivity (dBZ): Measures microwave energy bounced back by precipitation droplets. Green (20–35 dBZ) is light/moderate rain, red/magenta (>45 dBZ) indicates severe squalls or hailstorms.

2. Doppler Velocity: Uses frequency shift to detect particle velocity toward/away from the station, revealing wind shear, squalls, and cyclone circulation.

3. 0–3 Hr Nowcasting: Numerical weather forecast models take hours to run. Doppler radar scans every 10 mins to catch sudden cloudbursts before they hit.

4. Persona Integration: Directly powers Commuter waterlogging predictions, Agromet spray-hold warnings, and Playground lightning alerts.
